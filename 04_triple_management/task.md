# Scenario 4 — RDF Maintenance and Triple Management

## Situation

A catalogue maintainer has two versions of catalogue metadata and needs to inspect the changes before review.

## Your task

Open:

- `catalog-snapshot-a.ttl`
- `catalog-snapshot-b.ttl`

Review and maintain the two RDF snapshots as you normally would. Use RDFusion where it helps, and use any other tools you normally use.

Try at least two actions, such as filtering triples for one dataset, formatting or grouping Turtle, merging files, generating VoID metadata, comparing RDF versions, using visual IRI shortening, or normalizing Unicode escapes.

## RDFusion features to try

This scenario is intended to exercise Triple Management: filtering, sorting, grouping, formatting, merging, VoID generation, RDF diff/comparison, visual IRI shortening, and Unicode normalization.
